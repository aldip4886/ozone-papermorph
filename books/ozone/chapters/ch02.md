## Errata (decided)

- The workbook's matching exercise says "Match the institution and its function (COP or Secretariat)" but lists three bodies; the practice matches all three (COP, Research Managers, Secretariat).
- "Ratification … through which a State establishes … consent to be bound": the quick check asks when a state formally agrees to be bound, not when it is "legally bound" (that also needs entry into force).
- Added from the Convention itself (Art. 17): entry into force 90 days after the 20th ratification.
