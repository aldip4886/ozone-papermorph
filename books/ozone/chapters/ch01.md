## Errata (decided)

- Workbook (p. 10) says Cl "splits an ozone molecule back into an oxygen molecule and an oxygen atom". The real first step is Cl + O₃ → ClO + O₂, then ClO + O → Cl + O₂. The lesson shows the real cycle.
- Workbook (p. 11) gives global warming potential "per molecule" (CFC-11 "over 4,500 molecules of CO₂"). GWP is per kilogram, not per molecule; the lesson says "a kilogram of CFC-11 warms more than four and a half thousand times as much as a kilogram of CO₂". Bars use AR4 100-year values (4 750, 10 900), labelled "> 4 500", "> 10 000".
- Workbook (p. 8) says the 1992 hole covered "almost 60 million square kilometres". NASA's largest daily areas are about 25–30 million km²; the lesson shows the growth without area figures (sizes illustrative).
- Workbook answer key "Construction" counts as a sector (foam building insulation) and "Agriculture" (methyl bromide); kept.
